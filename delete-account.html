Delete Your Account

Last updated: March 2026

JidouNavi allows you to delete your account and associated data at any time. This page explains how to request account deletion and what data will be affected.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the JidouNavi app on your device
  2. Go to the Profile tab
  3. Tap the Settings icon (gear icon)
  4. Scroll down and tap "Delete Account"
  5. Confirm your decision when prompted

Warning: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Data That Will Be Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data will be permanently removed:

  • Account information: Email address, username, and profile photo
  • Photos: All vending machine photos you uploaded
  • Visit history: All your check-ins and visit records
  • Badges and XP: All achievements and experience points
  • Saved machines: Your saved/bookmarked machines list
  • Upvotes: All votes you cast on machines
  • Verification records: Your machine verification history

Data That May Be Retained

The following data may be retained after account deletion:

  • Machine submissions: Vending machines you added will remain in the database as community contributions, but will no longer be associated with your account
  • Anonymized analytics: Aggregated, non-identifiable usage statistics

Processing Time

Account deletion is processed immediately. All your personal data will be removed from our systems within a few seconds of confirming deletion.

Alternative: Contact Us

If you are unable to access the app or have trouble deleting your account, you can request account deletion by emailing us at jidou.navi@gmail.com. Please include the email address associated with your account.

Questions?

If you have any questions about account deletion or your data, please contact us at jidou.navi@gmail.com.
